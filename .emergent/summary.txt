-   **Financeiro (Financial):** Manage contracts (installments or single payment), track income and expenses, and view cash flow by month, semester, and year.
-   **Publicações (Publications):** Integrate with court APIs (TRT6, TJPE, JFPE) to receive updates based on lawyers' OAB numbers or names.
-   **Atendimento (Client Service):** Register client interactions, link them to a client and case, and record the client's request.
